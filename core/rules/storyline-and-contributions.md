# Storyline, motivation, and contribution design

Write one testable thesis before a full draft: under what conditions does a particular technical relation limit the task, what operation changes that relation, and what existing evidence supports the change?

Use one compact map:

| Specific problem/assumption | Technical change | Available evidence | Supported claim | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Actual input/representation/learning condition | Defined operation, not a module name alone | Source-located comparison, proof, or measurement | Local technical conclusion | Counterexample/untested regime |

A motivation chain must pass through **difficulty → reasonable route → hidden assumption → current mismatch → consequence → technical requirement → method response**. “Data are scarce, therefore we add three modules” skips the scientific reasoning. An experimental observation used as motivation must already exist; do not present post hoc analysis as a pre-registered hypothesis.

Contributions describe an object, a substantive change, a capability, and support. Default journal-method organization: framework/problem formulation, core mechanism, task-aware design with validation. Use the actual number of independent contributions; do not manufacture a third by splitting one idea. A minor block, largest ablation drop, or best rank alone does not prove independent novelty.

Maintain a four-column correspondence: introduction question → contribution → method subsection → evidence paragraph. Remove unsupported novelty and unverified “first” claims. Related work compares actual assumptions and operations at the same level; do not invent deficiencies in all prior work to make the story convenient.

Draft in a dependency order suited to the material. Method/evidence before final introduction/abstract often reduces rework, but a user-requested abstract or introduction can be drafted immediately from verified materials. The [outline template](../templates/outline-template.md) and section blueprints turn the map into paragraph tasks.
