# Venue mechanics and content profiles

Read only the target/profile in scope. Reuse known target information. A missing target stays unknown; do not silently choose TIP, TPAMI, or a conference. [ieee-facts.md](../templates/ieee-facts.md) points to scoped official sources. Current official requirements constrain house conventions, and scientific accuracy constrains all wording.

## journal_method_structured_v2: the detailed default

For IEEE-style journal method papers, apply the complete section blueprints: nine abstract functions; eight introduction functional paragraphs with sentence tasks; problem-oriented Related Work with compact second paragraphs; method title/figure/symbol/equation mapping; topic-first experimental paragraphs and E1–E6 operational analysis; a supported concluding thesis. See [sentence-plan.md](../templates/sentence-plan.md).

These are prescribed writing defaults selected for this suite, not official IEEE requirements. If actual content or the target's hard limits require adjacent merges, document the function mapping. Do not delete motivation or mechanisms or invent a module to fill a slot. Default three substantive contributions, actual independent contributions prevail.

A typical section order is Introduction, Related Work, Method, Experiments, Conclusion. Roadmaps are useful when they improve navigation. No fixed body percentage, page-one teaser, exact caption length, compulsory cost study, mandatory diagnostic figure, or per-module experiment is imposed by this profile. Use existing evidence required by the actual claim.

## Other journal paper types

Theory: definitions, assumptions, claims, proof, and scope. Systems/hardware: constraints, interfaces, platform/workload, measurement and actual resource costs. Application/measurement: sampling and measurement validity, error sources, actual use case. Adapt the same problem→method→evidence→boundary chain; do not force a learning-network contribution pattern or invent performance tables.

## Conference adaptation

Retain factual grounding and explicit functional structure, but use the current **year/track** instructions and official kit for CVPR/ICCV, NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, AAAI, or another named target. Do not reuse old page counts, blanket anonymity assumptions, or IEEEtran. Compress adjacent abstract/intro functions when space requires and identify their carrier sentences. A short Related Work positioning close remains concise; the target can permit integrated placement. Methods may use run-in headings instead of deep nesting. Captions remain sufficient to interpret the figure/table; arbitrary length or color ceilings are not universal rules.

A venue norm used as a finding needs a source. A corpus habit is advisory. For actual hard caps, anonymity, required statements/checklists, supplementary accounting, and links, create a scoped target record from current official guidance. Unknown mechanics do not stop substantive writing, but prevent a “submission-ready” verdict.
